First time here? A one-minute guide
  1. Choose a permitted JPG, PNG or video and upload it. For video, select a timestamp in seconds.
  2. Detect and number subjects locally. Correct missing or incorrect boxes, then inspect the exact context and crop previews. Uploading and previewing do not send it to ChatGPT.
  3. Connect your ChatGPT plan, acknowledge the selected image transfer, then choose Send to LLM.
  4. Read the observations, suspected issues and uncertainty. Save your own review decision and export the JSON.

LLM's visual assessment can be wrong. One selected frame does not establish speed, permitted travel direction, or legal guilt. The local detector workflow is optional below.
